01Do I collect your data?

No, I don’t collect any user data. These tools are developed to help fellow Admins perform their day-to-day tasks with ease. They are built with Google Apps Script, which runs on the V8 Engine — the most secure JavaScript Engine.

Whatever data you download or upload using these tools goes directly to the Google Workspace Admin Console using the required APIs to perform actions (like Add/Delete/Suspend/Update Users, Groups, or Attributes). Upon completing the action, it gives confirmation strictly to the user.

All of the above is done using a secure OAuth pipeline, ensuring that data reaches the specific intended user only (the person actively using the tool).

02Can I see or access your data?

No, I cannot access or see your data. Your data is stored entirely within Google Sheets under your ownership. Unless you specifically share that sheet with another user, not only me, but no one else can see your data.

03Conclusion

Dear Users,

I can’t access or see your data. It remains securely in your Google Sheet under your full control.

These Add-ons are created transparently with Google Apps Script. I have published snippets of the code working behind these tools on my GitHub Profile to maintain full transparency with the community.

Questions about your privacy?

I’m happy to explain how the add-ons work. You can also read the terms of use.
