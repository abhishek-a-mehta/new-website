Google Workspace add-on

Bulkyduce

The ultimate Google Workspace™ Add-on for bulk user and group management directly inside Google Sheets.

4.5 rating 708K+ installs Workspace Marketplace
Install from Marketplace All add-ons

About

This add-on can perform actions such as

  • Download, Check Duplicates, Create, Update, Suspend & Delete Google Workspace™ Users in bulk.
  • Export, Create, Delete Aliases of Google Workspace™ Users in bulk.
  • Export, Create, Update, Delete Google Workspace™ Groups.
  • Export, Add, Remove users from Google Workspace™ Groups.
  • Update Custom attributes of GCPW™ for Enhanced Desktop Security.

How to install?

One-time setup

  1. Go to sheets.new and log in with your corporate email ID.
  2. From the menu above, click Extensions (or Add-ons) › Get add-ons.
  3. Search for Bulkyduce.
  4. Click Individual Install / Domain Install › Continue.
  5. Click Allow to grant the necessary permissions.

How to use it?

Post-installation steps

  1. Reload the same sheet where you installed the add-on.
  2. Go to Extensions › Bulkyduce › choose your desired action (e.g. Download users).
  3. All the users/groups will be processed in the next few minutes.

Note: You can reuse the same Google Sheet to perform continuous actions.
