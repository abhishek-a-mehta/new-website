Frequently asked questions

Do the add-ons collect or store my data?

No. The add-ons don’t collect any user data. Whatever you download or upload goes directly to the Google Workspace Admin Console through the required APIs, over a secure OAuth pipeline — and the results stay in your own Google Sheet. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I install an add-on?

Open sheets.new with your corporate account, go to Extensions (or Add-ons) › Get add-ons, search for the add-on, choose Individual or Domain Install and click Allow. Each add-on has a step-by-step guide above.

Can I reuse the same Google Sheet?

Yes. You can reuse the same sheet for future actions without repeating the setup.

What do I need to use the add-ons?

A Google Workspace account. The add-ons work on your behalf through Google’s OAuth, so they can only perform the actions your own account is allowed to perform in the Admin Console.

Are the add-ons free?

Yes — they’re free Google Workspace Marketplace add-ons, built to help fellow admins.
